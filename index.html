P.A.T. + ICT + Retest = market-structure trading with BOS/CHoCH classification, liquidity-sweep tagging, multi-zone retest confirmation, and a real structural stop-loss.
H1 = swing-structure trend bias, fading toward neutral over time since the last break.
M1: a fresh BOS/CHoCH matching H1 arms a setup. Three retest zones are then watched — the OTE Fibonacci zone, a zone around the original broken structure level, and a projected trendline from the last two swing points. The setup fires only when price touches one of these zones and the touching candle confirms with a wick rejection or a strong momentum candle, and the resulting entry price is still on the correct side of the stop-loss (see fix note in script).
Stop-loss is placed just beyond the swing point that broke (the setup's own invalidation point), plus a small buffer (SL BUFFER × ATR). If price reaches SL before TP1–3, the trade closes there — losses are capped, not left open until a full structural reversal.
On trigger, three take-profit levels are set at 1×, 2×, and 3× the TP step (ATR(14) on M1) from the confirmed entry price. Each level alerts once when reached.
